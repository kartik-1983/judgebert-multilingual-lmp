## Important scope notes

- Selective starter index only; not comprehensive and not legal advice.
- The U.S. catalogue is federal; state law is especially important for contracts, insurance and privacy.
- The Canada catalogue is federal; provincial and territorial laws may materially change the result.
- UK law differs across England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland; check territorial extent.
- German federal law coexists with Länder law and directly applicable EU law.
- French and Spanish entries are linked to national official portals; verify the current consolidated version, amendments and effective dates.
- Some category entries are thematic navigation aids rather than standalone plain-language statutes.
- Summaries are editorial and do not replace reading the official instrument.
- This catalogue is not used to train or validate the LMP model.

## Primary portals

- **India — India Code / Legislative Department**: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ — Central and state Acts; use Gazette for notifications and commencement.
- **United States — U.S. House Office of the Law Revision Counsel**: https://uscode.house.gov/ — United States Code; federal statutes only. State laws are separate.
- **United States — eCFR / GovInfo**: https://www.ecfr.gov/ — Federal regulations and Federal Register materials.
- **United Kingdom — The National Archives — Legislation.gov.uk**: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ — UK Acts and statutory instruments; territorial application must be checked.
- **Canada — Government of Canada — Justice Laws Website**: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/ — Consolidated federal Acts and regulations; provincial/territorial laws are separate.
- **Germany — Federal Ministry of Justice / Federal Office of Justice — Gesetze im Internet**: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ — Federal statutes; Länder law and EU law may also apply.
- **France — Légifrance**: https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/ — Official French legislation, codes and Journal officiel.
- **Spain — Boletín Oficial del Estado (BOE)**: https://www.boe.es/legislacion/ — Official State Gazette and consolidated national legislation; autonomous-community law is separate.
- **European Union (cross-cutting) — EUR-Lex**: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/ — EU treaties, regulations and directives that may apply alongside national law.
